02UTILITY

What role is JWX Token intended to play?

The whitepaper describes JWX Token as a utility token. Proposed uses include access to AI tools, staking, governance and contribution-based rewards. These functions explain the token’s intended role in the ecosystem; the source site does not clearly establish which functions are currently live for every item.

A two-year lock is described for the technical team. Foundation reserves are intended to remain locked for three years and then be released progressively. Operational tokens are described as being managed by the foundation according to need. Before an economic decision, these statements should be compared with current smart-contract, wallet and governance data.

The existing website states that 365 million JWX are circulating and 135 million are locked. Those figures are reproduced as a published snapshot. Without a linked contract address and current explorer evidence, they should not be understood as independently verified real-time values.
